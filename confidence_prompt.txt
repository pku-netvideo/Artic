Ask yourself: "If I were to trace the outline of the target object, would I have to guess where the edges are?"

- If NO (Edges are razor sharp): The evidence is solid.

Assign a "Readability Score" (Float 0.0 - 1.0).

- **0.6 - 0.8:** PASSABLE. Readable but edges are rough.
- **0.8 - 1.0:** PERFECT. Crystal clear, HD quality.
